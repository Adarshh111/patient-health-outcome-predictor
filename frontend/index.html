How HealthPredict Works

A simple workflow connecting patient information, machine learning and analytical results.

01. Enter Patient Information

Enter the required demographic, admission, treatment and medical-history information.

02. Generate Prediction

The Flask backend sends the patient information to the trained Random Forest model.

03. View Results

View the predicted readmission outcome and estimated probability through the dashboard.
