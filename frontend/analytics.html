About the Prediction Model

HealthPredict uses a Random Forest classification model trained on patient demographic, admission, treatment and medical-history related features.

The target variable is based on the patient's readmission status. A value representing readmission within 30 days is treated as the positive class.

The model output provides a predicted outcome and an estimated probability of readmission.
